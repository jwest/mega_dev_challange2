The three problems

parser: month read first · 374 reports · ticket

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day up to 12 comes out swapped: “booked 7 April, invite for 4 July”. Engineering shipped fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Check its scope before you tell a tenant it is fixed: our day-first tenants include en-GB and en-IE, and “eu locales” may not cover en-GB.

hint: booking-v3 form hint · 33 reports · ticket

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and reads the date day first. People type what the hint says. Only reports that came through the form of a booking-v3 tenant belong here: 3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it. Engineering answered “won’t fix: booking-v3 form retired”, but the tenant directory still lists these tenants on booking-v3. Until they move, it keeps happening.

shift: one day off · 5 reports · ticket

Not a swap at all. The date moved by one day, or two people in different places got different days for one meeting. That points at time zones, not date order. The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but the old table filed them under parser by tenant. No fix covers them.

For the desk

  • A new date report: check the tenant’s form and the dates in the body before you merge. Swapped day and month on booking-v3’s form is hint; swapped elsewhere is parser; one day off is shift.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the browser: it is wrong on 23 parser, 15 hint and 5 shift reports here.
  • Same subject does not mean same problem. Read the body when the subject is generic.
